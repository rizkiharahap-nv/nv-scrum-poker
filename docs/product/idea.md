# Idea: Scrum Poker (team planning poker)

## One-liner
A real-time, poker-table-styled planning poker room where the team collaboratively estimates sprint tickets in story points.

## Why now
The team needs a shared, fun way to agree on story points during sprint planning. Existing tools (planningpokeronline.com) work but are flat lists; a real poker-table feel + gamification makes the estimation ritual stick.

## Who
Internal team (Ninja Van sort operations + adjacent squads). No public audience.

## Problem
Story-point estimation in sprint planning is either ad-hoc or boring. Estimates get rubber-stamped because the ritual isn't engaging, so points drift and planning accuracy suffers.

## Constraints
- Web app, real-time multiplayer (multiple players vote in the same room).
- Tailwind-only styling (no .css/.less/inline styles), matching team convention.
- Internal tool — no auth for guests beyond an invite link (host creates room, guests join by link).
- Phase 1 scope is deliberately small (S appetite).

## Appetite
**S** (Phase 1) — bare MVP. Future phases (M/L) add poker-table dressing + gamification.
